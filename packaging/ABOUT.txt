----------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT IT DOES
----------------------------------------------------------------

  GhostCam sits between your webcam and every other app. It finds
  your face in real time and covers it (mosaic, black box, ghost
  or CENSORED bar), then publishes the protected feed as a
  virtual camera that Discord, Zoom, Teams, OBS and browsers can
  all select.

  Features
    - Real-time face detection, GPU accelerated (DirectML)
    - Cover styles: mosaic / black / ghost / censored
    - PARANOID fail-safe: covers everything if no face is found
    - Tactical HUD overlay, scanline and glitch filters
    - Overlay editor: add your own text and images, drag to place
    - Watermark: GHOSTCAM // your name
    - Procedural cockpit controls with interlocks and a
      MASTER KILL
    - Pop-out monitor and system-tray operation

----------------------------------------------------------------
  PRIVACY
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Zero telemetry. All face detection runs locally on your own
  machine, and no frames are ever stored or transmitted by
  GhostCam -- your video never leaves your computer.

  The one network call GhostCam makes is to GitHub, to check
  whether a newer version exists. It sends nothing about you,
  and you can switch it off in CONFIG.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
  - A webcam
  - OBS Studio installed (only for its virtual-camera driver;
    OBS itself does not need to be running)
